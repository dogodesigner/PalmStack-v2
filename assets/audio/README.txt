Custom audio is optional; a short synthesized victory tone is included in code.

Place the selected recording at assets/audio/win.mp3, then set only the win
entry in AUDIO.files in config.js to "./assets/audio/win.mp3".
The clip is limited to AUDIO.maxWinSeconds (8 seconds by default).
Pause preserves position. Mute changes gain without stopping playback.
New round stops and resets playback. Hiding the page pauses it.
The final recording has not been selected or included.
